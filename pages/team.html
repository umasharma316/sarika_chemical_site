First Generation · Founders
Founder

Suresh Sharma

Founder & Director

One of the founding pillars of Sarika Chemical, Mr. Suresh Sharma co-established the business in 1989 with a clear vision: to deliver dependable, high-quality packaging materials to the textile industry. His deep understanding of materials, supplier relationships, and manufacturing operations laid the groundwork for what Sarika Chemical is today.

With over three decades in the packaging industry, he continues to guide the company's manufacturing standards and long-term business strategy.

Manufacturing Supplier Relations Business Strategy Accountability Customer Success
Co-Founder

Vijay Sharma

Co-Founder

Mr. Vijay Sharma co-founded Sarika Chemical alongside Suresh Sharma in 1989, bringing strong commercial acumen and a customer-first philosophy to the business. His focus on building lasting client relationships and ensuring consistent quality delivery helped establish the brand's trusted reputation in Bhiwandi and Murbad's textile hubs.

His emphasis on transparent dealings and reliable supply has defined Sarika Chemical's approach to business, contributing significantly to the company's reputation for the past 35 years.

Client Relations Quality Assurance Business Development Trade Networks Diversification
Second Generation · Leadership
2nd Generation

Sumit Sharma

Director — Sales & Expansion

Carrying forward his father's legacy, Sumit Sharma joined Sarika Chemical and has been instrumental in expanding the business beyond textile packaging into bakery, stationery, and industrial sectors. He oversees day-to-day operations, manufacturing oversight, and new product development.

Sumit has been a key driver behind the introduction of modern in-house printing machinery capable of up to 6-colour output, elevating the company's branding and customisation capabilities.

Sales Business Development New Markets Pan India Network Diversification
2nd Generation

Ronak Sharma

Director — Operations & Client Relations

Ronak Sharma heads sales, marketing, and customer relationship management at Sarika Chemical. With a strong understanding of client needs across textiles and industrial packaging, he has been central to growing the company's PAN India supply network and onboarding new customer segments.

His approach combines the family's tradition of trust and personal service with modern business development practices, helping Sarika Chemical reach clients in every corner of India.

What Drives Us

Values We Live By

Passed from founders to the next generation — the principles that have shaped every decision, every order, every relationship.

01
Trust Above All

Every business relationship we build is grounded in honesty, consistency, and a handshake that means something. Decades of repeat customers prove it.

02
Reliability

When we commit to a delivery, we deliver. Our manufacturing setup, stock management, and logistics are built around never letting a customer down.

03
Continuous Growth

The second generation didn't just inherit a business — they evolved it. New industries, new printing capabilities, new markets. Growth is a habit, not a goal.

04
Family & Partnership

We treat our customers as partners, not transactions. A family-run business understands the value of long-term relationships in a way no corporation can replicate.

05
Quality Control

In-house manufacturing means we never outsource our standards. Every product — from MDF boards to BOPP bags — is checked before it leaves our facility.

06
National Reach

A PAN India supply network built over three decades. Whether you're in Mumbai, Delhi, or Chennai — Sarika Chemical delivers.
