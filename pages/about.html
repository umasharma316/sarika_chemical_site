Our Story

Three Decades of Excellence

Founded in 1989 by Mr. Suresh Sharma, sarika chemical has grown from a dedicated textile packaging business into a multi-industry packaging powerhouse.

How It All Began

A Family Legacy in Packaging

Established in 1989 by Mr. Suresh Sharma, sarika chemical has grown into a trusted name in the packaging industry, known for quality, reliability, and long-term business relationships.

"What started as a dedicated textile packaging material business has today expanded by second generation — Sumit Sharma — into multiple industries including textile, bakery, stationery, and industrial packaging solutions."

Based in Bhiwandi and Murbad, Maharashtra, sarika chemical operates with strong manufacturing infrastructure, modern machinery, and advanced in-house printing capabilities.

Our Journey

Milestones of Growth

From a small textile packaging operation to a PAN India supply network — every decade brought new capabilities and new markets served.

1989
Foundation
sarika chemical established by Suresh Sharma in Maharashtra, focusing on textile packaging materials.
1990s
Infrastructure Growth
Expanded manufacturing base in Bhiwandi — added machinery for MDF board cutting and HDPE bag production.
2000s
Bakery & Stationery Expansion
Diversified into cake and bakery packaging, and stationery products, reaching new customer segments.
2010s
Second Generation Leadership
Sumit Sharma joined the business, bringing modern processes and expanded printing capabilities.
Today
PAN India & 6-Colour Printing
Full in-house 6-colour printing, bulk production capacity, and supply network spanning all of India.

Our Infrastructure

Advanced Manufacturing & Printing

Our facilities in Bhiwandi and Murbad are equipped with modern manufacturing equipment and the latest in-house printing machines capable of up to 6-colour printing, enabling premium-quality packaging with excellent finishing and customized branding.

Every product passes through quality-controlled processes before leaving our facility — ensuring consistency whether you order 100 units or 100,000.
